# UP-02A và UP-02B — Source metric semantics

Hai prerequisite độc lập, có thể dùng chung source compatibility release nếu cần. Chỉ sửa qua authorization riêng sau D-A14. E-01/E-02 và source inventory ở [preparation](preparation-result.md). No historical transforms thiếu provenance.

## UP-02A — Occupancy normalization

- **Inputs/outputs:** SUMO 1.27.1 baseline và getter contract đã xác nhận cho supported TraCI client. Inbound lanearea và lane raw là percent [0,100], pass through và giữ current aggregate rounding. Outbound lanearea và lane raw là percent, chia 100 đúng một lần trước ratio aggregation, fullness threshold và rounding. Không suy ra unit từ magnitude. Unknown version/unit hoặc failed/NaN/out-of-range getter gắn unavailable reason, không tự clamp invalid thành measured zero. Giữ fallback chỉ theo approved completeness rules, ghi measurement method và unit version. Partial required lane group là unavailable trong group đó; full fallback chỉ khi có toàn bộ approved fallback population và provenance.
- **Components:** Visualize/observation/detector_manager.py:approach_occupancy_pct, outbound_occupancy_ratio; snapshot_provider.py:_build_direction, _spillback_metrics; canonical_cycle.py và contracts/traffic_observation.py chịu quality/serialization. de/silver/fact_builders.py và canonical reader giữ namespace/version/nullable quality. Kiểm tra Visualize/integration/projector/v1_projection.py (occupancyRate, hasSpillback) và AI_Control_Traffic_Light/environment/reward_calculator.py (spillback penalty) với corrected inputs. Không đổi controller policy hay training logic trong UP-02A.
- **Tasks:** (1) xác nhận exact raw interface units từng supported client/server version, cố định unit map; (2) thay heuristic ở cả bốn nơi, preserve partial/missing distinctions; (3) emit `occ_percent_v2` source semantics trong immutable run manifest/config provenance đã verify, expose source/method/quality cho Gold; (4) đối chiếu end-to-end fixtures và consumers. Direction occupancy % và spillback ratio là hai output semantics riêng. Old run/version thiếu provenance → OCCUPANCY_SEMANTICS_UNKNOWN, không merge với corrected.
- **Acceptance:** 0, .5, .8, 1, 1.1, 50, 100% → inbound same percent và outbound 0,.005,.008,.01,.011,.5,1 trước aggregation/rounding; test actual getter, below/at/above threshold, all/partial failures, fallback, finite ranges, canonical/Silver values, realtime projection và AI inputs. Pressure calibration chỉ sau gate, không lấy calibrated threshold từ probe. Exit source owner sign-off và independent oracle trên cả bốn paths.

## UP-02B — Queue measurement và provenance

- **Decision:** topology hiện tại 17/17 mixed, E2 movement mapping rỗng 85/85. Chọn `route_stopped_length_v1` cho population phù hợp sau oracle. Không force E2-only và không auto-switch per observation giữa hai methods cho official series. Alternate `e2_jam_movement_v1` chỉ cho compatible lane geometry và adopted independent cohort. Không coi E2 zero là missing hoặc bằng positive fallback.
- **Input/output:** archived approach lane membership/geometry hash, archived routing/movement policy, stable stop threshold, distinct vehicle IDs trong sensing scope, raw speed/length/route and quality. Route method sum (observed stopped vehicle length + configured 1.5m allowance) theo movement rồi lấy max movement. Đây là stopped-length estimate trong mapped approach, không phải full-link contiguous jam. Chỉ 0 nếu measurement complete và không có stopped queue. Getter/movement failure, geometry mismatch/unknown hoặc incomplete population → unavailable/quality flags. No partial estimate as official. Direction payload provenance gồm method ID/version, geometry/scope reference và quality; Gold descriptor binds source semantics, scope, exact window/sample lattice and measurement method. Không mix source methods trong một distribution. Cross-window comparability requires equal approved descriptors or explicit reviewed mapping.
- **Components/tasks:** snapshot_provider.py:_build_direction, _infer_movement và detector_manager.py:approach_queue_by_movement; configuration lane/geometry archive, canonical_cycle.py, contracts/traffic_observation.py (preserve measurement provenance), Silver fact builder and public bundle. (1) Pin scope/geometry and route rules; (2) source estimate and explicit quality, unchanged stop threshold unless separate approval; (3) expose method/geometry/version in canonical provenance and verify Silver roundtrip; (4) compare against independent raw detector/vehicle trajectory oracle across the 17 approaches, full/mixed routing, topology change, missing/partial, E2 measured zero vs positive stopped estimate, queue growth/max and nearest-rank 60/300. Keep blocked if geometry or movement oracle fails. Realtime queue projection regression also required.
- **Versioning and rollout for both packages:** adopt a typed optional measurement-provenance extension and manifest source semantics version (direction occupancy method, queue descriptor and quality, network outbound method). Existing V1 event ID and correction/conflict semantics remain unchanged. Follow strict validators: reader/schema compatibility support rolls out before producer emits any new fields. Implementation must prove canonical→Outbox→Raw/Bronze→Silver→public reader, plus Projector, accepts both historical and new metadata, with explicit absence/unsupported behavior. If additive extension is rejected, require a separate owner-approved contract versioning plan before emission. Existing events are never rewritten and updated payload only emits under new immutable run/source-semantics scope. Roll back by disabling new semantics/emission in new runs, preserve evidence and quarantine incompatible scope. No historical scaling, no per-row method mixing, no production migration on conditional entry.

Shared guard: measure source capture overhead at 0.01s step, 1Hz emission, payload bytes and regression risk to realtime. Fixture-only exit proves algorithm behavior. Official adoption requires provenance and cross-layer verification. [SA and FA acceptance](acceptance.md).
