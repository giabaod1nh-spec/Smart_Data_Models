## Locked invariants

- Raw preserves original bytes/identity and commits only after confirmed contiguous persistence.
- Bronze owns semantic duplicate/conflict state and durable change/invalidation history; ClickHouse MergeTree is not uniqueness authority.
- Silver owns canonical validity/materialization and frozen obligations; conflict suppression is immediate and invalidation mirror can recover independently.
- Silver public feed remains contiguous, source-bound and bounded; current validity row is not a complete history oracle.
- Gold ACK is distinct from window work; invalidations are a separate lane; work uses exact current-effective inputs, source fence and generation CAS.
- No cross-database atomicity, unlimited retention or arbitrary historical snapshot semantics.

These are compatibility constraints inherited from accepted phases, not newly granted performance targets.
